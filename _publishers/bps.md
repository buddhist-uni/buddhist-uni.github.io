Founded in 1958 in Kandy by A.S. Karunaratna, Richard Abeyasekera, and [Ven. Nyanaponika Thera](/authors/nyanaponika), the BPS became one of the premier publishers of English-language, Theravāda Buddhist material during the 60s through the 80s and 90s during the massive growth in interest from the anglophone world.

Starting in the 1990s, much of their catalogue was made available for free online, first by volunteer transcribers and eventually by the BPS themselves.
Most of this free catalogue was taken offline by a botched website rewrite in 2026, but a significant portion can still be found below.
