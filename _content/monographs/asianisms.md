> At the core of this book is a seemingly simple question: What is Asia? The answer involves an investigation of the multifarious discursive and material constructions of Asia within the region and in the West.

This edited volume traces the modern invention of "Asia" as an assumed cultural sphere, starting with the discovery of Asia's shared Buddhist heritage and ending with the contemporary discourse around Asia's growing role in the global economy.
