> Some chapters, such as those on the introduction of Buddhism into China and Buddhism in modern China, discuss their subjects synchronically. But other chapters, such as those on the major scriptures, worship of buddhas and bodhisattvas, and festivals and rituals, do not refer to any specific historical time and thus discuss their subjects diachronically.

An excellent, academic overview of Chinese Buddhism.
