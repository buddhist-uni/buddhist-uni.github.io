> Findings presented in these papers will be indispensable for scholars who are interested in tracing how regulations of monastic life develop or change across languages, cultures, and geographical locations.

This volume examines how Buddhist monastic regulations and related precepts were transmitted, interpreted, and practiced across medieval Asia.
Its fifteen scholarly contributions consider textual traditions and monastic communities in South, Central, and East Asia, showing how local contexts shaped the application of Buddhist discipline as it spread across Asia
