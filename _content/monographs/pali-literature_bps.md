This volume contains three classical works: 
1. The Pali Literature of Ceylon by G. P. Malalasekera (1928)
2. The Pali Literature of Burma by Mabel Haynes Bode (1909)
3. The Pali Literature of South-East Asia by H. Saddhātissa (1990)

All three works, previously published independently, are here compiled into a single volume.

A. P. Buddhadatta wrote a few corrections to Malalasekera's *Literature of Ceylon* in the *University of Ceylon Review* 5, no. 2 (October 1947): 56-61. [It can be found here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BDsQC2dDvm-9iM1HXQD15O_DXsWx6igj/view?usp=sharing) archived from [the University of Peradeniya library](https://web.archive.org/web/20241112185032/http://dlib.pdn.ac.lk/handle/123456789/972).
