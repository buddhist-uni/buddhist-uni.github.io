> We surveyed 13,122 adults across six countries in Asia... three countries in which Buddhists make up a majority (Cambodia, Sri Lanka and Thailand); two countries with Muslim majorities (Malaysia and Indonesia); and one country that is religiously diverse (Singapore).

This wide-ranging study of Southeast Asian religious beliefs, practices, and identities contains many interesting nuggets of insight into the similarities and differences across the region.
