> In the end, there is little compelling evidence that the shrine caves we see today were designed with meditating monks in mind.

Sharf challenges interpretations of Buddhist cave art in western China that assume the caves were primarily settings for monastic meditation. Focusing especially on Mogao, he considers how archaeological, textual, and visual evidence instead point to religious tourism, public worship, and private memorials as the main uses for such elaborately-decorated caves.
