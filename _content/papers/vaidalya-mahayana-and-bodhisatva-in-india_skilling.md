> This essay presents some thoughts about ‘Mahāyāna’ and ‘Bodhisatva’ in India during the early period, from about the third/second century BCE up to the fourth/fifth centuries CE.

A long and somewhat rambling exploration of what the early Mahāyāna was, what it wasn't, and how it related to its non-Mahāyāna antecedents and contemporaries.
