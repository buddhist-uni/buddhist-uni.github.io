> What is particularly striking is that texts of the Mahāparinirvāṇa-group do not merely imply 
that *tathāgatagarbha* refers to something like an account of the self, but they declare that the Buddha unambiguously explains it to be the self!

A series of inspirational similes in the Tathāgatagarbhasūtra intended to inspire confidence in the immanence of Buddhahood inevitably gave rise to many different doctrinal explanations of what exactly was meant by this quality.
