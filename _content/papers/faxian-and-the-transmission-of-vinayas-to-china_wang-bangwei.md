> Although some Vinaya texts had already been introduced in China prior to Faxian's travel to India, not a single Vinaya text was complete. This had affected the development of Buddhism in China.

> Without Faxian, the *Mahāsāmghika* vinaya and the *Mahīśāsaka* vinaya could have been lost.
