> To the bodhisattvas, The arhats and so on...

The Early Mahāyānists rewrote the homage to the Triple Gem to fit their new doctrines and to put the Bodhisattvas first.
At least here Vasubandhu still includes the "Hīnayāna" even if in second place, unlike [Mātṛcetra](/content/essays/praise-triple-gem_matrcetra) who refused to include the Śrāvakas at all.
