> ... all monks, whether passersby or visitors, are treated and served in accordance with the law. There is no this side nor that side. There is no host nor guest. Monks are equal in principle and protect in unison the Buddhist law.

> Daoxuan focused on the *upoṣadha* as the key monastic rite for the regeneration of a communal life in Chinese monastic communities, which were 
segmented into independent, family-like groups.
[...] It is in this context that Daoxuan reorganized the fragmentary sources on the *sīmā* in the Vinaya into a consistent 
principle of *sīmā* determination, adjusting them to the reality of China.
