> We found that the word orders speakers used in their everyday speech did not influence their nonverbal behavior.
> Surprisingly, speakers of all four languages used the same order and on both nonverbal tasks.
> This order, actor-patient-act, is analogous to the subject-object-verb pattern found in many languages of the world and, importantly, in newly developing gestural languages.

This study suggests that SOV word order is the most natural and provides surprising evidence against linguistic relativity.
