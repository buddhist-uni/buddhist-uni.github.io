> One state, the one with the Aṣṭa identity, somewhere 
decided to call itself the Great Vehicle and dissociated itself from the Small Vehicle and the idea caught on in some 
other states who joined the bandwagon, even 
though it is entirely possible that many of those 
communities were not founded on the Aṣṭa principle. The end result is a hotch-potch, not really 
very united, 'Mahayana' front that gives the semblance that there is one entity called 'Mahayana.'

The Mahāyāna was not formed as a coherent school or ideology but was rather a set of different movements and trends that, in different ways, sought to sidestep rising sectarianism.
