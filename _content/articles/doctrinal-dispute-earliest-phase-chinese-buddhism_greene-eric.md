Eric M. Greene argues that the *Scripture on the Fifty Contemplations*, traditionally attributed to An Shigao, was instead composed in China in the second or third century. Its critique of Mahāyāna soteriology offers evidence that Chinese Buddhists were already critically comparing Buddhist teachings and debating the Buddhist path from an early date.
