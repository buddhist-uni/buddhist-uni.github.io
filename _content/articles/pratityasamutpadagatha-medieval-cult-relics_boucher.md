> The *ye dharmā* verse had become more than an epitome of the Buddha’s enlightenment. It became a manifestation of the Buddha’s real presence at cultic centers.

The verse on dependent origination came to be inscribed on stūpas, clay seals, and relic objects in early medieval India (c. 600–1200) as a way of making the Buddha present at the site through a "Dharma Relic."

This use of the written word of the Dharma for its consecrational value continues today across the Buddhist world, from the prayer flags of Tibet to the Khom Yantras of Thailand.
