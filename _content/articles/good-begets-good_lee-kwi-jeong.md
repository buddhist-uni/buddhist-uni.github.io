> They sought to prove not simply the compatibility of the foreign religion and Chinese traditions but also the universal applicability of the Buddhist notion of merit beyond religious and cultural denominations.

Kwi Jeong Lee examines sixth-century Chinese attacks on the Buddhist doctrine of merit as being materialistic and the Buddhist responses preserved in apologetic writings. By reading these exchanges, the article shows how disputes about karma and merit expanded Chinese Buddhist teachings.
