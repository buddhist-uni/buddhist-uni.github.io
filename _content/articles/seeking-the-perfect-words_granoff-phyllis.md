> In Buddhist and non-Buddhist stories transmission is a ritualized contest between a master and a disciple, usually involving the humiliation of the disciple and, with the exception noted in the *Śaṅkaradigvijaya*, virtually never involving the actual transmission of a recognized body of knowledge.
