> The participants felt that the government would have intervened if mercy release were harmful to the environment; if it were not outlawed, it followed that the act was not harmful, therefore they had no responsibility for any consequences.

> Whenever I gently suggested that mercy release harmed both the wildlife and the environment, all participants were quick to reject my suggestions and reiterate that they were doing a good deed.
