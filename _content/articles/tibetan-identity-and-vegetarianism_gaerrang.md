With many Chinese Buddhists criticizing Tibetan Buddhists for their consumption of meat, more and more Tibetans are becoming vegetarian.
Some Tibetans feel that this is losing an aspect of their culture while others feel like it is making them better Buddhists and thus preserving their identity, highlighting the complex relationship between religious and ethnic identity in Tibet.
