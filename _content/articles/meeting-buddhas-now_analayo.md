Bhikkhu Anālayo examines the development of meditative encounters with Buddhas and Buddhafields through a comparative reading of Chinese Āgamas, Pāli discourses, and early Mahāyāna Sūtras.

The article traces the recollection of the Buddha, visionary encounters involving Śākyamuni and his disciples, and the emergence of the idea of multiple Buddhas and their Buddhafields. It provides important historical background for understanding the emergence of the Mahāyāna from earlier Buddhism.
