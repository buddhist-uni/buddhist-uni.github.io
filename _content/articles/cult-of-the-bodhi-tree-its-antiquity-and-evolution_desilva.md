> By recognising the *bodhimaṇḍa* as the navel of the earth, which  extends up to the cupola of the Brahma world forming an impregnable vacuum like the hollow of a drum, the Buddhists have made the Upaniṣadic counterpart of a Cosmic Tree of life, the axis uniting heaven and earth.

While Buddhists primarily worship the Bodhi Tree for its role in protecting the Buddha on the night of his Awakening, the *Ficus religiosa* — *aśvattha* — was a sacred tree in Indian mythology long before the Buddha.

> Perhaps this latitude in popular Buddhism was considered permissible as it only served to enhance  the magnanimity of the Buddha, and the majesty of all that was associated with the Supreme Victory
