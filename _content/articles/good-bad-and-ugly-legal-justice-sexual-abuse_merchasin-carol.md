> Having a team of lawyers who believe you and fight for you can aid in recovery. So can holding perpetrators and their enabling institutions accountable. So can recovering compensation.

While the Saṅgha (ideally) has the Vinaya to guide it, it's important to remember that the Saṅgha also exists within a secular legal system which can provide some redress for abuse.
