> The final stage in the development of the cosmological image of hell is reached at the moment when the Eight Great Hells, which developed from the Mahāniraya concept, are combined with the Arbuda series.
> This does not mean, however, that from this point onwards no variations are possible.
> Quite the contrary: how the various elements are combined varies greatly from text to text.

Van Put traces how Buddhist cosmology evolved from the single *Mahāniraya* ("great hell") of the Āgamas into the complex system of "Eight Great Hot Hells," supplementary *utsadas*, and the *Arbuda* cold hells found in later Chinese Buddhist texts.
The tour covers the offenses, tortures, Sanskrit and Chinese names, and lifespans of each infernal realm.
