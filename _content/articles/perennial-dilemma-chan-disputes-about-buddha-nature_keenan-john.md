> The texts of the *tathāgatagarbha*
lineage in India, however, made a countermove. They restricted the 
scope of emptiness, leaving outside its purview the really real reality 
of the seed/womb (*garbha*) of a buddha (*tathāgata*) that lies secure beneath the surface of our consciousness; it is that consciousness that is 
the deluded field needing to be emptied.

Keenan traces how Indian Mahāyāna explorations of emptiness shaped the early Chán teachings of Shénxìu (神秀), Shénhùi (神會), and the *Platform Sutra*, showing how Chán debates over gradual versus sudden enlightenment looked in light of earlier doctrinal disputes over the fundamental nature of the mind.
