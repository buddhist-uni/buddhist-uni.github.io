> Complex community dynamics, ineffective institutional responses, and broader societal forces of systemic violence intersect to further the impact of individual victimization.

There are nine different kinds of violence. Physical, sexual, and social violence may be perpetrated domestically, communally, or systemically.
Seeing and understanding these nine types of violence and how they interact is critical for understanding how some forms of violence are contained while others persist.
