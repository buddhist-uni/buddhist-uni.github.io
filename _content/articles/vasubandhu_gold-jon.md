> His master work of Abhidharma thought, the *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya*, is to this day the primary resource for knowledge of non-Mahāyāna philosophy among Tibetan and East Asian Buddhist schools. His concise works on Yogācāra philosophy set a new standard for that school [...] This article will sketch his contested biography and works, and provide summary analyses of his most prominent arguments.
