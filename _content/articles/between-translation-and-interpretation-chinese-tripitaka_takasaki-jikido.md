> Particular to Paramārtha's translations are his insertions of sentences explaining his own doctrinal interpretation as well as quotations from other texts to support this interpretation

Unlike Buddhism's transmission everywhere else, when the Buddhist texts came to China, they were not kept in their source language but were translated into Chinese, often with considerable creativity.
