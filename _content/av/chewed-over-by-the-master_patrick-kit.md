> Kumarajiva hated those [early] translations. He said that listening to one of these Chinese translations of the Sanskrit texts was like eating food that someone’s already chewed: Not only has it lost all its flavor, it’s likely to make you sick.

Special episode R of Season 3 of *The History of India Podcast* tells the story of the life of Kumarajiva: one of the great translators of Buddhism into China.

The episode is a fine and entertaining retelling, even if he does call [Charles Patton](/authors/patton-c) "an early form of Google Translate" 😅
