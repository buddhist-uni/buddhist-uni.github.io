> I didn't realize the dimensions of the entire subject at that time, but I did immediately realize that this was a major discovery.

In this interview with Daniel Aitken, Richard Salomon introduces the ancient Gāndhārī Buddhist manuscripts found in what is now Pakistan. He explains how scholars deciphered the language and what these roughly 2,000-year-old texts reveal about early Buddhist literature, the development of Buddhist canons, and the transmission of Buddhism into Central and East Asia.

> Someone asked me, 'Did you find a fifth noble truth?' And I said no... We don't find anything that fundamentally undermines our general belief about what Buddhism was all about... but we do find a lot of interesting new twists and presentations of that material.
