> This was the only stupa in all of India at this time irregular in this way.
They had to make some of the pillars thinner than the others to make it all appear slightly regular.
The whole thing was a huge fudge made of stone.
You can imagine the workers dreading news from the capital, dreading a messenger coming with a new command.
And what would have happened if the patrons themselves, the rich men of empire, came to visit their own caves?
Well, let's find out! 🪘
