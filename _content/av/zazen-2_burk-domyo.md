> Can you sink below the level of thinking and become aware of your direct experience of each and every moment, without wavering? So you don’t miss a thing?

In this second part of her [introduction to Zazen](/content/av/zazen-1_burk-domyo), Domyo Burk discusses what we are training when we practice Zen meditation and encourages us to put in the effort it takes to do nothing.
