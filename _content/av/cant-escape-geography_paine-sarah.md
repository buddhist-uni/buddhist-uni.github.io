In this fast-paced and wide-ranging lecture, Sarah Paine, of the US Naval War College, outlines her theory that continental empires pursue security by conquering or disrupting their neighbors while maritime powers can pursue prosperity through trade. And because conquest is value-destroying, empires built on trade are richer and stronger in the long run than empires built on conquest.
