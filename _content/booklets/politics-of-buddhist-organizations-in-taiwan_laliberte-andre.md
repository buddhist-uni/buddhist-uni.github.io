> This dissertation looks at the political behavior of three Taiwanese Buddhist organizations since 1989: the Buddhist Association of the Republic of China (BAROC), the Buddha Light Mountain monastic order (or Foguangshan) and the Buddhist Compassion Relief Tzu Chi Association (or Ciji).
> It concentrates on trying to understand the rationale behind the different strategies that each of them has adopted in its interaction with the government.

This dissertation was edited and published commercially by Routledge with the subtitle [Safeguard the Faith, Build a Pure Land, Help the Poor](https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203505830){:target="_blank"} in 2004, with those three imperatives mirroring these three organizations' goals.
