Beyond the assumed "male/female" gender binary and the assumption that the one is always sexually attracted to the other (called "cisnormativity" and "heteronormativity" respectively), there are a vast rainbow of other ways of being engendered and being attracted (or not!).
Queer studies is the study of these alternative genders and sexualities specifically and generally is the study of how messy and strange social categories and assumptions really are.

Here you'll find works on LGBTQ+ history and queer theory which go beyond what you might get in an introductory gender studies course, but that remain approachable to the general, interested reader.
