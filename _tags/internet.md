The global "network of networks" running on TCP/IP allows billions of computing devices
around the world to communicate with each other in near real time.
Since the proliferation of smartphones and cellular data, most humans on Earth have access
to the net, with an ever-increasing number spending significant time "in cyberspace"
consuming traditional and [social media](/tags/social-media) or producing "intellectual property."

In this tag, you'll find works primarily discussing the social implications of this major shift
in communications technologies.
