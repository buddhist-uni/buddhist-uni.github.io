*Dāna*, or giving, is a foundational, ethical practice in Buddhism.
When we practice giving, whether things, service, or time, we lay the
ground for an altruistic attitude and we also begin learning how to
"let go" of what we think is "ours".

Here, you will find works about the many ways to practice charity in
Buddhism as well as its karmic, social, and psychological effects.
