Sotāpanna is the initial breakthrough to the Dhamma in Theravāda Buddhism that comes with a guarantee of eventual enlightenment and non-rebirth in the lower realms.
This makes it the most important milestone for Shravakas.

In this tag, you'll find works about the stages leading up to stream entry as well as about what characterizes the "opening of the Dhamma Eye" both doctrinally and phenomenologically.
