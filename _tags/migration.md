title: "Human Migration"

---

People relocate over long distances both inside and between countries with the intention of living somewhere else for a while or permanently for a large variety of reasons.
There are "pull" factors that draw people to particular places (such as economic opportunities) as well as "push" factors that drive people to leave their current communities (such as climate change).
Two million years ago, Homo erectus walked out of Africa, and today there are humans living on every continent, including Antarctica.

The works in this tag explore the long history of human migration, its contentious present, and its possible futures.
