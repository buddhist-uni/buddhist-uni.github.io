New Age spirituality is a highly eclectic set of occult beliefs and
practices which became popular in the 1970s in the West as the 1960s
counterculture sought a religious grounding for their pursuit of
liberation and "human potential."
Many such Western "seekers" have made their way to Buddhism,
bringing with them their unique mix of syncretic beliefs and attitudes.
