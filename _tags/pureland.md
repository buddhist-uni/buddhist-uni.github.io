A large branch of the Mahāyāna tradition, Pure Land practitioners are so called because they strive for rebirth in a transcendent realm created and sustained by a particular Buddha's power, most commonly Amitābha's.
Pure Land practitioners believe that it's guaranteed to get [enlightened](/tags/nibbana) in such a Pure Land and that it's easier, thanks to the Buddhas' great merit, to get reborn there than it is to get enlightened here, making the Pure Land a popular "short cut" to Nirvāṇa for those who believe in its cosmology.

In Japan, several Pure Land schools developed as independent religious sects, while in China and Tibet, Pure Land practitioners typically belong to the same institutions as those practicing other methods.
