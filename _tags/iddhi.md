Buddhists believe that when certain people attain mental concentration,
they can develop supernormal abilities such as to read minds,
recall past lives, see or hear at a distance, fly through the air,
communicate with spirits, etc.

Here, you'll find books about what these powers are, how they work, how to
develop them, and various beliefs around them from across the Buddhist traditions.
